# Floating Eye Software

Floating Eye Software (FLEY) is a small Toronto software practice for public
web projects, games, tools, and lightweight quality-system work.

This organization is the current home for active FLEY product and operations
repositories. Some long-running game and research repositories still live under
Michael Lehotay's personal GitHub account while their long-term home is being
decided.

## Public surfaces

- [Pancakes](https://www.pancakes.ca/) is the main public product surface for
  rituals, care, service exchange, and community governance.
- [Floating Eye NetHack](https://www.floatingeye.net/) is the public
  roguelike service and project front door.
- [Non-Kitten-Item Press](https://nkipress.floatingeye.net/) is the publication
  surface for IER and related writing.

## Repository map

- Product repositories such as `pancakes`, `redwitch`, and `pitchfork` live
  here when they are ready to be organization-owned.
- `site-ops` tracks domains, hosting, deployment mappings, public-surface
  inventory, TLS, redirects, and live-site verification.
- `fley-org` is the authority for organization topology and cross-repository
  coordination.
- `fley-qms` contains the lightweight quality management system used to keep
  governance separate from day-to-day engineering execution.
- NetHack-related source such as
  [NetHack](https://github.com/mlehotay/NetHack),
  [dgamelaunch](https://github.com/mlehotay/dgamelaunch), and
  [bonegraft](https://github.com/mlehotay/bonegraft) remains under
  [mlehotay](https://github.com/mlehotay) for now, even though it is part of
  the public Floating Eye story.

Pinned repositories below are entry points, not the full map.
